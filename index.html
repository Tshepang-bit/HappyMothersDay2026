Dear Mom

There are no words grand enough to describe what you mean to me. I know I can be a headache and problematic sometimes but you still choose to love me unconditionally. You have been my safe place, my greatest cheerleader, and my very best example of what it looks like to love without limits. Every memory I treasure has your fingerprints on it. Thank you for everything you've done for me and everything you've helped me to accomplish. I love you more than you'll ever know.

— Your child 💕
